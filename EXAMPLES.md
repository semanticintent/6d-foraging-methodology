## Featured Case Studies

### 1. Tailwind CSS — AI Disruption Cascade

**Multiplier:** 7-11× | **Dimensions:** 5 of 6 | **Industry:** Open Source Software

Tailwind CSS demonstrates the AI disruption pattern: 75 million monthly downloads, yet revenue collapsed 80%. AI coding assistants now bypass the documentation funnel that drove monetization, creating a paradox where adoption and revenue became inversely correlated.

**Key Learning:** OSS sustainability crisis as AI trained on open source bypasses the monetization mechanisms that sustain development.

### 2. Aviation MRO — Parts Inventory Cascade

**Multiplier:** 18.5× | **Dimensions:** 6 of 6 | **Industry:** Aviation Maintenance

What appeared as $119K in operational inefficiencies cascaded to $2.2M in total impact across quality, customer relationships, revenue, and regulatory compliance. The highest multiplier in our case study collection.

**Key Learning:** Operational issues rarely stay operational—they cascade through quality to customer to revenue.

[**Read full case study →**](cases/aviation-mro.md)

---

### 3. Startup Pivot — Market Shift Cascade

**Multiplier:** 1.6-2.3× | **Dimensions:** 5 of 6 | **Industry:** B2B SaaS

Market consolidation created revenue concentration risk when top 3 customers merged. Early detection and intervention limited cascade depth, demonstrating the value of proactive 6D analysis.

**Key Learning:** Even "contained" issues have cascade effects. Early intervention is critical.

## Pattern Library

Common patterns observed across case studies:

### The AI Disruption Pattern

```
AI automates discovery → Bypass monetization → Revenue collapse → Layoffs → Quality risk
```

**Seen in:** Tailwind CSS, documentation-driven businesses, SEO-dependent models

### The Concentration Pattern

```
Revenue concentration → Customer leverage → Pricing pressure → Margin erosion
```

**Seen in:** B2B SaaS, agency models, enterprise sales

### The Operational Ripple Pattern

```
Operational issue → Quality impact → Customer complaints → Revenue pressure → Cost cutting → Worse operations
```

**Seen in:** Manufacturing, MRO, logistics, service delivery

### The Talent Exodus Pattern

```
Uncertainty → Key people leave → Knowledge loss → Quality drops → More uncertainty
```

**Seen in:** Post-acquisition, leadership changes, market downturns

### The Compliance Cascade Pattern

```
Regulatory finding → Remediation investment → Resource diversion → Delivery delays → Customer impact
```

**Seen in:** Healthcare, finance, aviation, any regulated industry
