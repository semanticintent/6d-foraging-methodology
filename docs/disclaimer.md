## 3. Framework Limitations

### Analytical Boundaries

6D Impact measures:
- **Observable signals** in business systems
- **Cascade pathways** across six dimensions, written in cascade notation
- **Dimension scores** (0-100, from cited evidence) and a CAL **FETCH** score against a threshold

**What it does NOT measure**:
- Organizational culture and politics
- Leadership quality and decision-making
- External market forces
- Competitive dynamics
- Technology disruption risk
- Black swan events

### Scoring Methodology

Our framework uses:
- **Pattern recognition** (signal and keyword detection)
- **The 3D Lens** (Sound × Space × Time) as one way to build a dimension score
- **CAL scoring** ([spec v1.2.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24090152)): Chirp, DRIFT, Confidence and FETCH against a threshold
- **Counted patterns** from the published case library (counts of what earlier cases recorded, not probabilities)

The framework does **not** use cost multipliers, "total impact" dollar estimates, or cascade probabilities. Earlier versions did; those numbers were invented and have been removed.

These are:
- **Judgment-based**, not deterministic
- **Opinionated**, based on design decisions
- **Subject to error**, as with all analytical frameworks
- **Context-dependent**, requiring local calibration

**Always apply critical thinking** and validate insights with your specific organizational context before making major decisions.
